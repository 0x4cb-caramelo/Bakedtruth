## Every power and its limit

| Power                                                | Who                   | Limit in the program's code                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Replace the program                                  | The upgrade authority | On devnet, at any time and with no waiting period. A replaced program could do what this table rules out.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Open a campaign, paying                              | Any wallet            | US$500 in SOL at the Pyth SOL/USD price the transaction reads, less its confidence band; the kinds this build opens (four today: price, burn, locked liquidity and stake pool; validator stake waits for its first devnet proof); an account the program's reader accepts; 3 to 90 days; the fees copied from the config when it opens, never above the ceilings below. Refused once the cap on open campaigns is reached.       |
| Open a campaign without paying                       | One team key          | No prize and no creator share. It does not count against the cap on open campaigns. Used for the team and launch partners: a team rule, not a limit in the code.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Confirm a result in review                           | One team key          | Only while the campaign is in review, inside its 6-hour window. It never changes a YES or a NO.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Record the cause of an automatic void                | One team key          | Only on a campaign the program already voided by itself (a missed reading, an unreadable account, a stale price feed), inside its 6-hour window; never on a YES or a NO. It ends the review and sets the cause, which decides whether the 10% opening fee goes back to whoever opened or stays with Brot. The program stores the cause and the hash of the team's note; publishing each one in the public record is a team rule. |
| Open a contest                                       | Nobody yet            | The program refuses it until the dispute's second stage is built.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Pause new entries and openings                       | One team key          | Never results, reviews, payouts or refunds.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Resume after a pause                                 | The admin             | Only the admin: a team key can pause but not resume.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Change the creation parameters                       | The admin             | New campaigns only, within ceilings written in the code: Brot's fee at most 10% of the losing side, the creator share at most 1%, the one-sided fee at most 1%, an opening price of US$1 to US$5,000 with at least 90% as prize, a minimum entry of 0.001 to 1 SOL, at most 10,000 open campaigns. An open campaign's fees never change. A low cap stops paid openings while free team openings go on.                           |
| Change the team keys, the fee recipient or the admin | The admin             | A new admin takes over in two steps.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Resolve, settle and pay                              | Any wallet            | Chooses no result, amount or destination: the result comes from the account read, and every amount and destination from the program. On a price campaign that ends YES, the proof the resolver posts sets which entries count as late (made at or after the proof's time), and those come back whole.                                                                                                                            |
| Move Brot's fees                                     | Any wallet            | Only to the fee recipient.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Move or take a baker's entry                         | Nobody                | No instruction does it, and there is no emergency withdrawal. The upgrade authority could still replace the program (first row).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

## The plan for mainnet

The plan, if this ever runs with real money, is that the upgrade authority, the admin and the fee recipient move to a
2-of-3 multisig on hardware devices first, and that an upgrade then waits 24 to 48 hours in public before it takes
effect. This page changes with them.
