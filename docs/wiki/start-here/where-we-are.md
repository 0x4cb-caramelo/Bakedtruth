# Where we are today

> **In one bite:** the oven is on, the bread isn't ready. When it is, it's test money first.

**Last updated: 8 October 2026.**

- **Markets aren't open yet.** The Brot Markets program is being written. The first markets will open on Solana
  devnet, the test network, with test USDC that is worth nothing.
- **The program is not audited.** Until there's an audit report, every screen says so.
- **The waitlist is open** at [brot.markets](https://brot.markets). It keeps your email and nothing else, as the
  [Privacy Policy](https://brot.markets/privacy) explains.

## The three steps

| Step | What you'll be able to do | What has to be true first |
|---|---|---|
| **Now: devnet** | Take sides with test USDC in all five [market types](../market-types.md), open markets, get paid automatically | The program, its readers, the review window, automatic payouts and the app |
| **Launch** | Take sides in our launch partners' markets, each about the partner's own milestone | Partners signed. None is yet |
| **Mainnet** | Use real USDC, up to a cap | An audit, a multisig and a cap |

## Real money comes after three things

1. **An external audit** of the program. An audit doesn't prove there are no bugs, and it only covers the version it
   read, so later changes get reviewed again.
2. **A multisig.** The program can only be upgraded by 2 of 3 founders, each signing from a separate hardware device,
   and every upgrade waits for a public delay before it takes effect.
3. **A cap** on the total money in open markets. It starts low and goes up in steps, each after two weeks without an
   incident.

If any one of the three is missing, the date moves. Not the other way round.

## After mainnet

- **Disputes anyone can raise,** with a deposit. Today only our team can trigger one, and anyone can tell us.
- **More tokens.** Burn markets start with tokens on Solana's classic token program. Tokens on the newer Token-2022
  program come later, with a reader reviewed on its own.
- **Phase 2:** leaving a market before it ends, and sports, through an oracle of our own.
