Boa noite! Eu sou o Lucas, e estamos fundando o *Brot Markets*:
mercados de previsão que a *própria blockchain* resolve.

Num mercado de previsão, na hora H, *quem diz o que é verdade?*

Em março de 2025, *um único mercado de 7 milhões de dólares* na Polymarket

No Brot, só abre mercado que a blockchain responde.

[exemplo 1, o preço]

Aqui, o preço do SOL vem da Pyth. Acertei, e o dinheiro cai sozinho na carteira.

Aqui, a queima de um token, lida na conta do próprio token. Errei, e perdi a entrada.
*Ninguém escolhe o vencedor*, e qualquer um confere a mesma conta.

[exemplo 3: o texto da esquerda vira "10%"]

E é aqui que a gente dá a mordida: *10% do lado que perdeu*.
Num pote de 10 SOL, 6 acertaram e 4 erraram: a gente fica com *0,40*, e quem acertou divide *9,60*.

## Roteiro em inglês

<!-- en:inicio -->

*1 · COVER*

Hi, I'm Lucas, and we're building *Brot Markets*:
prediction markets that *the chain itself* resolves.

*2 · PROBLEM*

In a prediction market, when it matters most, *who says what's true?*

In March 2025, *a single 7-million-dollar market* on Polymarket
settled on an event that never happened. No refunds.

On Polymarket, a dispute goes to a token vote and takes four to six days.
On World, it's one company key. On PNP, two language models.

Someone always stands between the event and your payout.

*3 · SOLUTION*

Brot only lists markets the chain can answer.
If the answer isn't stored in a Solana account, the market doesn't open.

[example 1, the price]

Here, the SOL price is read from Pyth. I was right, and the payout lands in my wallet on its own.

[example 2, the burn]

Here, a token burn, read from the token's own account. I was wrong, and I lose my entry.
*Nobody picks the winner*, and anyone can check the same account.

[example 3: the text on the left turns to "10%"]

And this is where we take our bite: *10% of the losing side*.
In a 10 SOL pot, 6 were right and 4 were wrong: we keep *0.40*, and the winners split *9.60*.

[example 4: the text on the left turns to "1%"]

And *1% of the volume* goes to whoever opened the market, out of our fee:
*0.10* to the creator, *0.30* to us, and the winners keep their 9.60.

Any project can open its own, with a seed from *500 dollars*, and 90% of it becomes the prize.

*Proof, not promises.*

*4 · MARKET*

[the mascot runs from June to September; wait for it to stop]

Prediction markets moved *21.7 billion dollars* in 30 days.

On Solana this has barely started: world.xyz, the prediction market inside the Phantom wallet,

*Thirteen times in three months.*

*5 · PROJECTION*

[the words go, the chart pulls back and the mascot climbs]

And that is only the start of the curve.
In our scenario, Solana reaches 5% of today's category volume:
*more than a billion dollars a month*, two years from now.

*That's where we'll be.*
Anyone can open a market, and only the chain decides it.

To grow: *20 to 30 launch partners* with one free market each,
referrals paying *20% of the fee* for 90 days,
lower fees for the first three months,
and the 1% that makes every creator promote their own market.

Today, devnet. Mainnet only after an audit, a multisig and a cap.

*7 · TEAM*

Behind the oven: *Bernardo*, our CEO, who decides what goes in the oven;
me, keeping the oven running and the recipe legal;
and *Ronaldo*, our CTO, who wrote the recipes.

*8 · CLOSE*

*Proof > Promises.*

Let's build your startup's market together.
There are only *20 to 30* partner spots.

Scan the QR, follow *@brotmarkets* on X and DM us. Thank you!

## O que este roteiro não resolve

- **De onde sai o 1%.** O slide e esta fala dizem "tirado da nossa taxa" (0,30 para o Brot, 0,10 para quem abriu, 9,60
  para quem acertou), como o fundador pediu. O programa na devnet tira o 1% do pote, ao lado da taxa: 0,40, 0,10 e
  9,50. Enquanto as duas coisas não baterem, um jurado que leia o código encontra outra conta. O corte de 2 minutos
  não diz de onde o 1% sai, de propósito.
- **O bilhão por mês.** É 5% dos 21,7 bilhões da categoria, número escolhido para a prévia. Não há fonte para ele.
- **A semente de 500 dólares** só existe na fala: o slide que a mostrava saiu.
- **"Um teto" no slide de crescimento.** O plano de negócio v0.0.4 não tem teto; o slide ainda diz "a cap".
- **"World" e "world.xyz".** O slide 2 ainda diz "World, inside Phantom"; o slide 4 diz world.xyz.
- **Os sete logotipos de parceiros** do slide 6 são exemplos, e o cartão diz isso. Não há sete parceiros.
- **A Complian.cc** segue no slide de equipe; o site dela respondia "conta suspensa" em 10/10.
- **O PDF do deck** não mostra a taxa nem o 1%, que agora só aparecem com o demo rodando.
