# Privacy Policy

**Brot Markets** · Draft 0.1 · 8 October 2026 · Not in force yet: waiting for legal review. Until then, the page at
brot.markets/privacy, which covers the waitlist, is the one that applies.

Most privacy policies are long because the company collects a lot. Ours is short because we don't. We need almost
nothing about you to run a market, so we keep almost nothing.

> **The crumb:** we don't knead your data. An email if you give it, a wallet address that's public anyway, and that's
> the whole loaf.

## The whole thing in 30 seconds

- **Waitlist:** your email, the date you joined, and which version of the consent sentence you saw. Gone when you
  leave, or after 12 months.
- **Markets:** your wallet address and your entries. They live on Solana, which is public and permanent. We can't
  hide them or delete them, and neither can anyone else.
- **No** name, no ID, no password, no cookies, no analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels, no selling.

## 2. What we collect, and why

### The waitlist

> **The crumb:** one email, one date, one version number. Baked counted.

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your email address | To tell you when markets open on devnet, and again when they open with real money, if they do | Until you leave, or 12 months after you joined, whichever comes first. Old rows are deleted automatically |
| The date and time you joined | To know when the 12 months end | Same |
| The version of the consent sentence you saw | To prove what you agreed to | Same |

We ask for it on the form, and you give it by sending the form. That's your consent, and you can take it back at any
time by leaving the list. We don't sell, rent or share the list, and we use it for nothing else.

### Markets

> **The crumb:** the blockchain is a glass oven. Everyone can see what's inside.

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your wallet address, and the markets you entered, opened or got paid from | Solana records them; it's how the program works. Our site reads them to show "my entries", activity, and rankings | On Solana: forever, and public. In our database: see below |
| A copy of that public data, in our database | So the site loads fast without asking Solana for everything every time | As long as the site needs it. We can delete the copy; we can't delete the chain |

**Before your first entry, the site tells you this:** everything you do in a market is public and permanent, and
anyone who links your wallet to your name can see your history. If that matters to you, use a wallet you don't use
for anything else.

### Every visit

> **The crumb:** we look at your address for a minute to stop spam, then we forget it.

- **Your IP address** reaches our server because that's how the internet works. We use it for about one minute, in
  memory, to stop one machine from spamming the forms. We don't write it to disk or to our logs.
- **Your country**, when markets open with real money, may be checked the same way to block places on the restricted
  list (see the Terms, section 2). Also kept in memory only.
- **Our logs** record counts ("an email was added, total N"), never your email and never your IP.
- **Your browser** remembers one thing on its own: whether you picked the light or dark theme. It stays on your
  device, and we never see it.

## 3. Who else touches your data

> **The crumb:** two helpers, both named. No secret bakers.

- **Cloudflare** carries every request to our server. Like any network provider, it sees your IP address, under its
  own [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Cloudflare also tries to add its own visitor
  statistics to our pages; our site's security settings block that script, so nothing is measured.
- **Our server** is rented in the United States. Only the service that runs the site, and the founders who manage the
  server, can read the waitlist file.
- **Solana, and the wallet you choose** (Phantom or another), handle your transactions under their own rules. We don't
  control them.
- **An email service** will be needed to send the "we're open" email. We haven't picked one. This page will name it
  before the first email goes out.

We'll give data to the authorities only when the law requires it. There isn't much to give.

## 4. What we don't do

No cookies. No analytics. No ads, and no advertising partners. No tracking pixels in emails. No buying data about you
from anyone. No profiles. No selling, renting or "sharing with partners". No artificial intelligence trained on your
data.

## 5. Your rights

> **The crumb:** ask, and you'll get it. Usually in a single slice.

Depending on where you live (for example under the LGPD in Brazil or the GDPR in Europe), you can ask us to show you,
correct, export or delete your data, or to stop using it. In practice:

- **Leave the waitlist:** type your email at brot.markets/privacy. It is deleted at once. We keep no backup copy, so
  deleted means deleted.
- **A copy of your waitlist record:** ask us (section 1). It's three fields; we'll send them.
- **Delete our copy of your market activity:** ask us, and we'll remove it from our database. The record on Solana
  stays, because no one can delete it.
- **Complain to a data protection authority** in your country, like the ANPD in Brazil, if you think we got it wrong.
  We'd like to hear it first, though.

## 6. Security

The waitlist file can only be read by the service that runs the site and the founders who manage the server. There's
no admin panel and no page that lists the emails. The form answers the same way whether or not an email is on the
list, so nobody can use it to find out who signed up. If we ever have a breach that affects you, we'll tell you and
the authorities as the law requires.

## 7. Age

Brot Markets is for adults (see the Terms, section 2). If you're under 18, please don't join the waitlist. If we learn
that someone under 18 did, we delete it.

## 8. Changes

If we start collecting anything new, this page changes **before** we do it, with a new date at the top and a post on
X. If the change needs your consent, we'll ask for it. We won't just assume you said yes.
