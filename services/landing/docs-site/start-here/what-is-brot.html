What Brot Markets is

In one bite: a prediction market that only asks questions the blockchain can answer, and then lets the blockchain answer them.

Brot Markets is a prediction market built as a growth engine for on-chain projects. You pick a milestone the blockchain can measure, like a burn, locked liquidity or staked SOL, and sponsor a market on it. Your community takes sides, talks about it and shares it. At the deadline, the chain itself reads the result and pays the winners. No votes, no judges. Your milestone becomes a campaign that proves itself.

Who it's for

Projects. A project announces a goal the chain can measure, like "burn 100M tokens by Nov 30", and puts money behind it. Today a promise like that lives in a post, and nobody checks. In a market it gets a date, money on both sides and an answer read from the chain.

People who take a side. You connect a Solana wallet and put USDC on YES or NO. No account, no name, no ID.

People who open markets. Anyone can open a market, as long as it's one the chain can answer. The program refuses the rest.

What makes it different

  • Only questions the chain can answer. If the answer isn't stored in a Solana account, the market doesn't open.
  • A program reads the result. Nobody votes, and no person picks the winner.
  • Anyone can re-check. Same account, same answer.
  • One pot per market. Everyone's USDC goes into it, and the people who were right split it. There's no order book, no price to haggle over and no market maker. See The pot.
  • Automatic payouts. No claim button.

What it won't do

  • Opinion markets. No politics, no "will X happen", nothing that needs someone to say what happened. Why the chain decides explains the choice.
  • Early exit. Your money stays in until the market ends.
  • Hold your keys. You sign everything yourself, from your own wallet.
