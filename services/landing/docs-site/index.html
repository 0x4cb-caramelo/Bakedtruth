How Brot Markets works, in plain English. The serious part, with a few crumbs.

Where we are: markets aren't open yet. The first ones open on Solana devnet, with test USDC worth nothing, and the program is not audited. More on that.

What Brot Markets isA prediction market that only asks questions the blockchain can answer, and then lets the blockchain answer them.Why the chain decidesEvery prediction market needs a witness. We picked the one that can't be talked into anything.Where we are todayThe oven is on, the bread isn't ready. When it is, it's test money first.

How it works

The potEveryone's money goes into one pot, and whoever was right splits it. We take a slice of the losing side, never of the winners' stake.Life of a marketOpen, closed, in review, paid. And if something went wrong, annulled, with everyone refunded.How a market is decidedThe program reads, a human can only say "that's wrong", and nobody can flip a result.
