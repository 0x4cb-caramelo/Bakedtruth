03 · SOLUTION

Brot only lists questions the chain can answer

If the answer isn't stored in a Solana account, the market doesn't open. The program reads that account and settles. Nobody types in a result.

Wallet-only No account, no KYC sign-up.
USDC pools One pool per question, YES or NO.
Auto payout Winnings arrive in the wallet. No claim button.
04 · PRODUCT

Five steps from bet to payout

Example "Does SOL touch $300 by Dec 31?"
01 Bet 300 USDC on YES, 700 on NO. Screen shows 30% / 70%.
02 Close Betting stops before the deadline, at 5% of the duration left.
03 Resolve The program reads the Pyth price on-chain and records YES or NO.
04 Review 6-hour window. The team can confirm or void, never flip.
05 Pay USDC goes straight to the winners' wallets.
If YES: each 1 USDC on YES returns 3.10
If NO: each 1 USDC on NO returns 1.39
