04 · Open a campaign · permissionless

Anyone opens a campaign for $500 in SOL.

  1. Pick a target the chain can read: burn 100M tokens by Nov 30.
  2. Pay $500 in SOL, at the SOL/USD price the program reads from Pyth.
  3. Bakers join YES or NO, from 0.01 SOL.
  4. The chain answers. Payouts go out on their own.

90%of the opening becomes the prize.

10%is our opening fee.

1%of the pot goes to the creator.

Projects open campaigns on their own promises. Anyone else can too, inside the same on-chain rules. Launch partners open theirs free.
